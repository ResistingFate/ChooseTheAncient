No longer do you stumble upon the Ancients—now they come to you. At the end of each act, vote for the Ancient you want at the start of the next. [b]Compatible with Main 1.070.1 and Beta 0.110.0[/b]

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ResistingFate/ChooseTheAncient/refs/heads/master/workshop/Choose_The_Ancients_Custom_Short_Decision.gif[/img]
As you can see, you have three Ancients to vote for. After you select one, another Ancient has the chance to change your mind. The custom Ancient shown is [b]Arq's Ancients — Phoenix[/b] by Arquebus.

[h1]Features[/h1]
[list]
[*]After you start Act 1 or proceed to Act 2 or Act 3, a new screen asks you to choose the Ancient.
[*]Ancients' scenes clash together and zoom in as you hover over them.
[*]Multiplayer support. Votes are weighted, with randomized picks in multiplayer.
[*]After the first round, a second round begins where the second-most-voted Ancient clashes with the chosen Ancient.
[*]The second Ancient sweetens the deal by revealing their relic options, in case they have what you need.
[*]Proceeds cleanly to the next act, building the same rewards for the second-most-voted Ancient if you pick them.
[*]Ancient spawning is no longer determined by the base game.
[*]Darv can appear in both Act 2 and Act 3 during the same run.
[*]Works with custom Ancients.
[*]Controller support.
[*]Resizes for different resolutions.
[*]Optional settings:
[list]
[*]Number of Ancients
[*]Game modes:
[list]
[*]Monty Hall [i](default)[/i]
[*]Fair Fight
[*]I Want To Know Everything
[*]Simple Picker
[/list]
[/list]
[/list]

[h1]Mod Compatibility[/h1]
[list]
[*][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3737335127]BaseLib[/url] custom Ancients.
[*][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3747602295]RitsuLib[/url] custom Ancients.
[*]Use either BaseLib or RitsuLib for the full settings menu.
[*]Limited settings are available through [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3749062616]Mod Config[/url].
[*]Use [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3747493112]Ancient Configs Plus[/url] to filter Ancients. [i](BaseLib only)[/i]
[*]Works in endless mode, including [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3771500862]New Game ++[/url]. [i](Ascension 10 breaks; ACP only filters Act 1–3 bosses.)[/i]
[*]Act 4 mods do not have an Ancient node at the start, so no choice screen appears there.
[*]Usable with [url=https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/filedetails/?id=3750930021]Ancient Affection[/url]. However, the preview displays the “beloved” rewards under the wrong reward options.
[*][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3761888849]Slay the Streamer 2[/url] adds even more votes.
[/list]
